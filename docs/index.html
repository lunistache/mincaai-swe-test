01 · how it's graded

The rules of the ledger

Every vehicle record lands in exactly one of six outcomes. A wrong record that nobody flagged costs three times what a correct one earns — a confidently wrong answer is the expensive kind.

The review queue can absorb 20% of a file's records (minimum one). Flag inside that budget and it costs +0.10 — flag past it and every extra flag costs −0.25, whether the record was right or not. Blanket flagging is not a safe floor.
